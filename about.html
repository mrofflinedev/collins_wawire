COLLINS WAWIRE

Hi, I'm Collins Wawire, a Full Stack Developer based in Kenya.
My journey into technology began at Sivilie Secondary School, where a deep curiosity for computer studies sparked my passion for building things with code.
To understand the bigger picture of how digital solutions impact the real world, I joined Tom Mboya University to pursue a Bachelor's Degree in Economics with IT.
To sharpen my engineering edge, I am simultaneously undergoing intensive full-stack software development training at eMobilis Training Institute. I thrive at the intersection of business logic and clean code.
Whether designing intuitive user interfaces or structuring robust databases, my goal is to build secure, scalable applications that solve actual problems.
When I am away from my screen, you will likely find me exploring new landscapes through travelling or capturing stories through the lens of my camera (photography).
